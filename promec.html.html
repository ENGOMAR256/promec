About Our School

Building Strong Foundations for Tomorrow's Leaders

For over a decade, Promec Junior School has been committed to providing quality primary education rooted in discipline, innovation, and Christian values. Located on Gayaza Road in Kalagi, Kakoola, we prepare pupils P.1 to P.7 for academic success and life.

Our dedicated team of qualified educators uses modern teaching methods to ensure every child reaches their full potential — academically, socially, and spiritually.

Message from the Director

Leading with Vision, Integrity & Purpose

Mr.Bukenya Mansour Ngobya

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At Promec Junior School, we are committed to shaping young minds into future leaders who will transform our communities and our nation."

Welcome to Promec Junior School. As the founder and director, it is my honour to lead an institution that has been transforming lives through quality education for over a decade. Our mission is simple yet powerful — to provide every child with the tools, values, and opportunities they need to succeed in life.

We believe that every child is unique and possesses extraordinary potential. Our role as educators and parents is to nurture that potential with love, discipline, and academic excellence. At Promec, we don't just teach — we inspire, we mentor, and we build character.

I invite you to join our growing family and give your child the gift of a Promec education — where excellence is not just a goal, but a way of life.

Mr. Bukenya Mansour Ngobya

How to Find Us

By Car / Taxi

From Kampala, take Gayaza Road northbound. Pass through Kawempe, continue past Zana. Look for Kalagi/Kakoola signpost on your right. School is 200m after the junction.

By Public Transport

Take any Gayaza-bound matatu from Kampala (New Taxi Park or Wandegeya). Alight at Kalagi/Kakoola stage. Walk 200m towards the school gate.

Landmarks

Near Kakoola Trading Centre, off Gayaza Road. Close to Kalagi Market. Look for the blue and gold school gate with "Promec Junior School" signage.
